# POSITIONING & COPY GUARDRAILS

## Primary Positioning

**Elite Talent. Bulletproof Systems.**

Ash River Collective puts the right people in the right seats — then
builds the systems that make their work repeatable.

## Supporting Positioning

ARC combines trained remote talent with documented processes and
practical automation to help companies increase execution capacity
without adding unnecessary overhead or operational complexity.

## Messaging Priorities

1.  Lead with the work and business outcome.
2.  Position talent as part of an operating system, not generic
    outsourcing.
3.  Make Finance & Accounting Talent and Virtual Assistants the primary
    commercial offers.
4.  Use Systems & Playbooks as the differentiating mechanism across both
    offers.
5.  Emphasize fluent English, U.S. time-zone alignment, role-specific
    capability, and integration into the client's existing systems where
    applicable.
6.  Keep Ash River Foundation connected to the broader mission while
    clearly separating its workforce-development purpose from ARC's
    commercial services.
7.  Keep CTAs simple: Build Your Team, Find Talent, Talk With ARC,
    Become a Partner, or Partner With the Foundation.

## Matt-Style Copy Rules

-   Direct.
-   Operational.
-   Specific.
-   Short sentences.
-   Minimal marketing language.
-   No vague claims.
-   No inflated adjectives.
-   Explain the mechanism.
-   Focus on execution, ownership, capacity, and measurable business
    outcomes.
